# Homepage

## Purpose
Persuade and orient institutional visitors. Within seconds, the page should answer: what ShoreVest does, where it operates, why its platform is differentiated, how the strategy works, and where to go next.

## Direction
Keep the existing ShoreVest visual identity and photography. Refine rather than redesign.

## Priorities
1. Proposition first. Remove generic or redundant pre-headline labeling.
2. Maintain a strong but controlled hero; avoid an oversized landing-page feel.
3. Use the Firm section for proof of platform breadth.
4. Present Strategy as a flat institutional list, not interactive feature cards.
5. Present China Debt Dynamics as editorial research, with featured status signaled sparingly.
6. Keep motion restrained and secondary to reading.
7. Preserve all confirmed business claims and URLs.

## Avoid
Card grids, startup-style badges, decorative side tabs, broad hover shadows, gratuitous animation, and adding visual treatments that do not communicate information.
