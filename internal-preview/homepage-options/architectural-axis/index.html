Underwriting discipline

For a secured credit investor, the relevant question is not whether every part of the property market is healthy. It is whether a specific asset can generate cash, attract users and transact at a price that leaves the lender with a meaningful margin of safety.

Collateral value and margin of safety
Entry terms are assessed against downside cases and expected recovery value. Higher cap rates and recent transaction evidence should be reflected in collateral values.
Legal and structural position
Claim priority, documentation and enforceability are reviewed for each situation.
Exit and recovery routes
Potential repayment, restructuring and resolution routes are considered before capital is committed.

Risk management and downside analysis are applied across investments, at both the transaction and portfolio level.

Perceptions Versus Reality in China Real Estate

Shanghai or Ghost Town? A tale of two markets, and why the distinction matters for asset-backed credit.

China's residential development downturn is real. But it does not follow that every completed commercial property is empty, untradeable, or unsuitable as collateral. The data increasingly points to a more segmented reality: severe stress in new residential development alongside improving liquidity and stabilization in selected completed, income-producing commercial assets.
