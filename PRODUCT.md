# ShoreVest Website — Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is institutional LPs and prospective investors evaluating ShoreVest, its strategy, and its investment expertise during fundraising and diligence.

Secondary audiences are borrowers, media, and recruits. Their detailed needs and success criteria remain open decisions.

## Product Purpose

Establish ShoreVest as a credible institutional private-markets manager; clearly explain its China asset-backed private credit strategy; support fundraising and diligence; and demonstrate genuine investment expertise.

Success means visitors can understand the manager and strategy, assess real evidence of expertise, and find relevant information and inquiry or access routes. Quantitative success measures have not been defined.

## Positioning

ShoreVest's confirmed focus is China asset-backed private credit. The website should communicate that specialist focus and institutional credibility through factual content and genuine investment expertise.

Existing strategy copy in `strategy.html` describes asset-backed lending, asset restructuring, and debt resolution, with attention to margin of safety, claim priority, enforceability, and exit routes. These are repository descriptions to preserve, not newly validated investment claims. Do not strengthen claims or infer exclusive advantages from them.

## Operating Context

The public website supports institutional evaluation, fundraising, and diligence through firm and strategy information, team information, research, media coverage, contact routes, and investor access.

The repository contains English and Chinese public pages, published China Debt Dynamics issues and white papers, investor portal entry points, and recruitment pages. It also contains internal operating-platform and backend code; this product record concerns the public ShoreVest website and does not authorize changes to those systems.

The existing public site uses static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. `README.md` and `package.json` document its existing implementation and maintenance conventions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the ShoreVest name and logo.
- Preserve factual investment claims. Do not invent performance figures, assets under management, customer or investor endorsements, testimonials, benchmarks, or other investment claims.
- Preserve the bilingual English/Chinese structure and corresponding content parity.
- Preserve legal and compliance content, including investment-risk disclosures, privacy, cookies, terms, important information, and legal notices.
- Preserve existing analytics and consent functionality.
- Preserve existing live URLs unless the user explicitly approves a change. This includes public page routes, legacy routes, and published PDF asset URLs.
- Preserve working public information, inquiry, investor-access, and recruitment behavior; do not infer deployment readiness from the presence of backend scaffolds.
- The existing website is functional. The user's concern that it feels too AI-generated is reserved for later design work. Initialization does not authorize a redesign or prescribe visual solutions.

Open decisions: quantitative success metrics, detailed secondary-audience workflows, additional approved evidence or investment claims, and product-specific accessibility requirements.

## Brand Commitments

ShoreVest is the confirmed name, and the existing logo must be preserved. Approved brand assets are stored in `assets/brand/`, as documented in `README.md`.

Institutional credibility and genuine investment expertise are product goals. No new visual world, palette, typography, component system, or aesthetic direction is established by this record.

## Product Principles

1. Serve institutional evaluation and diligence first while retaining access for secondary audiences.
2. Explain the China asset-backed private credit strategy clearly and precisely.
3. Establish credibility through genuine expertise and existing evidence, with no fabricated claims.
4. Maintain bilingual continuity, legal and compliance content, analytics and consent behavior, and stable public URLs.
5. Keep product truth distinct from visual design decisions.
