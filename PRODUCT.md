# ShoreVest Website Product Context

## Platform
web

## Audience
The public website serves institutional investors and allocators, borrowers and financial institutions, advisers and counterparties, prospective employees, and media or research readers. Visitors should immediately understand that ShoreVest is a specialist in China asset-backed private credit with long-standing local execution capabilities and institutional risk-management standards.

## Primary goals
- Establish credibility quickly without sounding promotional, generic, or startup-like.
- Explain the firm, strategy, execution capabilities, research, team, and contact paths with minimal friction.
- Help institutional visitors distinguish ShoreVest's specialist China credit platform from generic private-credit managers.
- Make China Debt Dynamics easy to discover and read.
- Keep investor-portal and contact actions obvious without turning the site into a conversion funnel.

## Content constraints
- Do not invent or embellish investment performance, assets under management, track record, client relationships, regulatory status, dates, biographies, research claims, or transaction facts.
- Do not alter legal, compliance, privacy, regulatory, or investor-portal wording for style alone.
- Preserve published URLs and bilingual English/Chinese parity where applicable.
- Prefer verified, specific language over superlatives and generic marketing copy.

## Experience constraints
- Institutional, restrained, precise, and editorial rather than SaaS-like.
- The site should feel designed by a specialist investment firm, not generated from a landing-page template.
- Avoid decorative cards, pills, badges, gratuitous gradients, glass effects, oversized icons, floating UI, fake dashboards, and novelty motion.
- Use motion only where it clarifies navigation or state. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Maintain strong keyboard focus, readable mobile typography, adequate contrast, and predictable navigation.

## Brand commitments
- English typography: DIN 2014 where available, with the approved Barlow fallback used by the live site.
- Chinese typography: Noto Serif SC for Chinese brand/editorial text where the existing implementation specifies it.
- Core colors: Cinnabar #C93B2A; Blue-Green #3D7070; Ink Green #2E4A18; Xuan Paper #F2ECD8.
- Dawn Redwood Gold #C89A2E is secondary/ceremonial and should be used sparingly.
- Sharp geometry and rules are preferred to rounded card systems.
